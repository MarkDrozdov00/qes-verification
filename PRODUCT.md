# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary user is someone who received a digitally signed or notarized PDF and needs a clear answer about whether it can be trusted. Secondary users include legal and compliance professionals, banks, authorities, business partners, notaries, and Notarity customers who need access to the supporting technical evidence.

## Product Purpose

Signature Validator turns a PDF upload into an understandable, dimension-by-dimension trust assessment. Success means a non-technical recipient can understand the overall result immediately, while a specialist can inspect signatures, certificates, notary evidence, Notarity evidence, and the audit trail without the document being retained.

## Positioning

Explain digital document trust without collapsing distinct cryptographic, certificate, notary, and platform-record checks into one misleading verdict.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and credible. The interface should feel premium and approachable rather than governmental, alarmist, or overtly technical.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic admin dashboards, neon or cyber-security styling, decorative gradients, excessive equal-weight cards, dense certificate-data walls, proprietary Notarity asset imitation, and status treatments that rely on color alone. Unsigned and uncertain results must not look like failures.

## Design Principles

- Lead with an understandable outcome, then reveal inspectable evidence.
- Keep every trust dimension independent and never overstate certainty.
- Make prototype provenance visible wherever it affects interpretation.
- Treat privacy, accessibility, and recovery from errors as core workflow states.
- Use restrained, consistent interaction patterns so the document result remains the focus.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard operation, semantic structure, visible focus states, strong contrast, text-and-icon status communication, connected error messages, responsive reflow without horizontal overflow, and reduced-motion preferences.
